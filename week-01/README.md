# Week 1 — Sep 3

**DATA 201: Statistical Methods in Data Science**

## Topics

- **Machine learning and statistical learning foundations** — What statistical learning is and how it is used in data science.
- **Inference vs. prediction** — When the goal is to explain a relationship versus when the goal is to predict well.
- **Supervised vs. unsupervised learning** — Labeled outcomes versus finding structure without a response variable.
- **Regression vs. classification** — Quantitative outcomes versus categorical outcomes.
- **Problem framing and tradeoffs** — Choosing a question, a method, and an evaluation plan that fit the data and the goal.
